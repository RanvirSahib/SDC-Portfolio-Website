- **Categorization on Inquiries & WhatsApp**: Kept simple and clean — **Veg** or **Non-Veg**.

  - **Live Street Chaat Bazaar**: Golgappe with 5 waters, Dahi Bhalla, Papdi Chaat, Aloo Tikki, Sev Puri.
  - **Royal Mughlai & Dum Biryanis**: Veg Dum Biryani, Chicken & Mutton Biryanis, Kebabs.
  - **Pan-Asian & Continental**: Live Pasta stations, Pizzas, Noodles, Dimsums, Crispy starters.
  - **Live Tandoor & Barbecue Counters**: Sizzling hot tikkas, chaap, and kebabs prepared live in front of guests.
  - **Beverages & Lassi Bar**: Desi Malai Lassi, Mocktails, Kulhad Chai, Espresso bar.
  - **Royal Desserts & Mithai**: Live Kesar Jalebi with Rabri, Hot Gulab Jamun, Moong Dal Halwa, Kulfi Falooda, and Ice Cream bars.
- **Banquet Protocol**: Polished brass/copper chafing dishes, fine bone china crockery, uniformed courteous service staff, strict food safety and hygiene.

## 4. Technical Architecture: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS

### Technology Stack:
- **Framework**: **React 18 / 19**
- **Bundler & Dev Server**: **Vite** (instant hot module reload, ultra-fast builds)
- **Styling**: **Tailwind CSS** (configured with custom royal themes: Gold, Deep Burgundy, Luxury Emerald, Charcoal)
- **Icons**: **Lucide React** (clean, modern SVG icons for WhatsApp, phone, calendar, utensils, location, Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
